How this works

  • Only makes/models/years seen in the latest scrape appear — no guessing.
  • Year choices start at {{ min_year }}: Kenya (KEBS/BVRS) bars vehicles more than 8 years old from import, so the selectable range rolls forward automatically each year.
  • The Japan side is the closest exporter listing (same make, matching model tokens, year within ±1) — unmatched picks are reported as "no comparable import found", never force-matched.
  • Landed cost = exporter price + Roro freight (est.) + marine insurance + KRA duties (IDA/CET/RDL) + port, clearing and registration fees.
